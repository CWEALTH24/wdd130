Fun Time Surge Rafting

At Blue Surge Rafting Co., our purpose is to create safe, thrilling, and unforgettable white-water rafting experiences that connect people to the power and beauty of nature. We aim to inspire adventure, teamwork, and respect for the natural environment through guided river expeditions. We believe that sharing the thrill of white water rafting with others creates lasting memories and strengthens bonds between people. Also, we are committed to providing exceptional customer service and ensuring the safety of all our guests.

History

Our History Founded in 1998,Blue Surge Rafting Co.began as a small family-owned adventure company with one raft, a passion for the outdoors, and a dream of sharing the thrill of white water rafting with others. Nestled near some of the region's most exciting river rapids, the company was created by a group of lifelong river explorers who believed that adventure could bring people together while inspiring respect for nature.

In the early years, Blue Surge Rafting Co. offered only weekend rafting trips for local adventurers. Despite its modest beginnings, the company quickly earned a reputation for safety, professionalism, and unforgettable experiences on the river. Guests were drawn not only to the excitement of navigating powerful rapids, but also to the friendly guides who treated every visitor like family.
